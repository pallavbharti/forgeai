# Pacific Spares Co — Supply Agreement (PSC-26)

1. Minimum order value: USD 300 per purchase order. There is no handling fee; orders below the minimum are rejected.
2. Discount: a flat 7% discount on every order of USD 2,000 or more, applied to the order value.
3. Delivery: delivered free to any agent in Singapore, Busan or Shanghai. Other ports are charged at actual freight cost.
4. Payment terms: 30 days net from the date of invoice. A 2% early payment discount applies if paid within 10 days.
5. Warranty: 18 months from delivery. Turbocharger cartridges carry a 12-month warranty.
6. Returns: returns are accepted within 14 days only for parts supplied in error by Pacific Spares Co.
7. Lead times quoted in the catalogue are from order confirmation to dispatch at Singapore.
