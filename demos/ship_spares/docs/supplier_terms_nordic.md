# Nordic Marine Supply — Terms of Supply (Agreement NMS-2026)

1. Minimum order value: USD 500 per purchase order. Orders below this value incur a handling fee of USD 75.
2. Volume discount: 5% discount on orders between USD 5,000 and USD 19,999; 8% discount on orders of USD 20,000 or more. Discounts apply to the order value before freight.
3. Delivery: Ex-works Rotterdam. Delivery to vessel or agent is charged at cost plus 10%. Urgent "AOG-style" dispatch within 48 hours is available for an extra charge of USD 250 per order.
4. Payment terms: 45 days net from the date of invoice.
5. Warranty: 12 months from delivery or 6,000 running hours, whichever comes first. Warranty does not cover wear from improper fuel or lube oil quality.
6. Returns: unused parts in original packing may be returned within 30 days, subject to a 15% restocking fee.
7. Certificates: all main engine parts are supplied with maker's certificate of origin.
