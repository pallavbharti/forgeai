# Oceanic Shipping Ltd. — Business Travel & Expense Policy (HR-POL-04)

## 1. Pre-approval
All business travel must be pre-approved by the department head through PeopleDesk before any booking is made. International travel additionally requires approval from the Chief Operating Officer.

## 2. Travel class
Flights under 6 hours are booked in economy class. Flights of 6 hours or more may be booked in premium economy. Business class is permitted only for Vice Presidents and above. All bookings are made through the company travel desk.

## 3. Daily allowance (per diem)
Domestic travel: a per diem of INR 2,500 per day covers meals and local transport. International travel: a per diem of USD 60 per day. Hotel stays are booked by the travel desk and are not paid from the per diem.

## 4. Expense claims
Expense claims must be submitted in PeopleDesk within 30 days of returning from the trip, with original receipts for every item above INR 500. Claims submitted after 30 days require written approval from the Finance Controller. Approved claims are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle.

## 5. Non-reimbursable items
Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, and upgrades not covered by section 2 are not reimbursed.

## 6. Mileage
Use of a personal car for business is reimbursed at INR 12 per kilometre, supported by a trip log.
