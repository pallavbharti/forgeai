# Oceanic Shipping Ltd. — Leave Policy for Shore Staff (HR-POL-01, rev. 2026)

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to all permanent shore-based employees of Oceanic Shipping Ltd. It does not apply to seafarers, whose leave is governed by their employment agreements.

## 2. Annual leave
Permanent shore staff are entitled to 18 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 1.5 days per completed month of service. Employees in their first year may take leave only after completing 3 months of service (the probation period).
A maximum of 10 days of unused annual leave may be carried forward to the next calendar year. Carried-forward leave must be used by 31 March, after which it lapses. Leave encashment is not permitted except on resignation or retirement.

## 3. Sick leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate from a registered doctor is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days. Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

## 4. Casual leave
Employees receive 6 days of casual leave per year for personal matters. Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave and may not exceed 3 consecutive days.

## 5. Parental leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children and 12 weeks for any subsequent child. Paternity leave is 10 working days, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption. Adoptive mothers of a child below 3 months receive 12 weeks of maternity leave.

## 6. How to apply
All leave is applied for through the HR portal "PeopleDesk". Annual leave of more than 3 days must be applied for at least 14 days in advance. The reporting manager approves or rejects requests within 3 working days. Emergency leave may be applied for retrospectively within 2 working days of returning to work.

## 7. Public holidays
The company observes 10 public holidays per year, published by HR every December for the following year.
