# Oceanic Shipping Ltd. — Hybrid and Remote Work Guidelines (HR-POL-07)

## 1. Eligibility
Shore staff who have completed their probation period are eligible for hybrid work, subject to manager approval. Roles in vessel operations control and the 24x7 fleet desk are not eligible.

## 2. Hybrid schedule
Eligible employees may work from home up to 2 days per week. Tuesday and Thursday are mandatory office days for all teams ("anchor days"). Work-from-home days must be marked in PeopleDesk by Friday of the preceding week.

## 3. Core hours
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 IST, during which employees must be reachable on Teams, whether at home or in the office.

## 4. Equipment and allowance
The company provides a laptop and headset. Hybrid employees receive a one-time home-office allowance of INR 8,000, claimable within 60 days of hybrid approval. Internet costs are reimbursed up to INR 1,000 per month.

## 5. Data security
Company data may only be accessed over the company VPN. Public Wi-Fi may not be used for vessel or customer data. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## 6. Working from abroad
Working from outside India is not permitted, except for up to 10 working days per year with prior written approval from HR.
